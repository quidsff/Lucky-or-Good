Lucky or Good?

Find out what your record isn't telling you.

Sleeper leagues only, for now. Nothing to log in to; we only read public league data.

How this works

Points per game is what your team actually scores. The playoff line is the points per game of the last team that would make your league's playoffs if the season ended today.

Expected wins is how many games you'd have won if you played every team every week. Luck is your real wins minus that number. Positive means the schedule has been kind.

Teams in the shaded band around either line get On the bubble, because they are at the mercy of Lady Luck.
